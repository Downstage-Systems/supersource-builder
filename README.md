# Downstage SuperSource Builder

Design the background behind your ATEM SuperSource boxes in your client's colors, and get Companion smart buttons that run the whole look. A free Mac app from Downstage Systems.

## What it does

- **Smart Companion buttons:** one button per look. Press it and it works out the move from wherever you are. It lights up when that look is on air, turns amber while a move runs, and ignores presses until it lands
- **No dissolve over a person:** press a look while a camera is live and it hands in: SuperSource takes that shot full screen, cuts to itself where nobody can see it, then moves
- **Straight to Companion:** send the page directly onto a Companion page, or import a file
- **Brand colors in one click:** sample them from a logo or any image
- **Patterns and backgrounds:** a grid of patterns drawn in your show's colors, from subtle to full stage looks, plus gradients and Auto design
- **Built around your boxes:** 1 to 4 boxes, with logos, borders, box frames and baked-in shadows placed around them
- **Title bars, text and overlays:** add them to the background and drag them into place, with a Layers list to reorder or hide any part
- **Look packs:** start from a complete look in your brand colors instead of a blank background
- **Several looks per show:** keep a 2-box, a 4-box and a rounded look in one file
- **Everything the switcher needs:** export the stills and the eased macros for every move, or just the background, with switches for what goes on it
- **Read from your ATEM:** pull a switcher's setup in by its IP address instead of loading a show file

It doesn't replace ATEM Software Control or Companion. You still load the macros and stills with File > Restore, and Companion is still where you set up your connections.

## Download

[Download for Mac](https://github.com/Downstage-Systems/supersource-builder/releases/latest/download/Downstage-SuperSource-Builder.dmg) (version 2026.10.31, Apple Silicon, macOS 11 or later). This is a beta, so test it on a switcher that isn't in a show first.

Open the disk image and drag the app to Applications. Allow Local Network access if macOS asks, so the app can reach your switcher and Companion.

Smart buttons need Bitfocus Companion with a Blackmagic ATEM connection. It's built for the ATEM Mini Extreme ISO, 2 M/E and 4 M/E.

Some patterns, frames, bars, overlays and look packs unlock when you enter your email in the app. The app tells you when there's an update, and nothing installs until you press Update now.

Keep a backup of your show file before File > Restore, since a restore can replace macros already on the switcher.
